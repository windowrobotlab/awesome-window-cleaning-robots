## 🛡️ Suction Safety & Glass Thickness Standards
Safe operation of automated window cleaners depends heavily on motor suction power and glass thickness:
* **Vacuum Pressure Thresholds**: Minimum 2500 Pa required for frameless vertical surfaces to prevent drop accidents.
* **Double / Triple-Glazed Glass**: Sensor calibration guidelines for edge detection on insulated glazing units.
* Detailed calculation matrices and fail-safe safety benchmarks are documented at [Window Robot Lab Safety Tests](https://windowrobotlab.com/).

## 🤖 Navigation & Path Planning Algorithms
* **N-Path vs. Z-Path**: Comparative efficiency on large panoramic windows vs. framed residential glass.
* **Dual Ultrasonic vs. Optical Edge Sensors**: Preventing perimeter slip and frame collisions.
